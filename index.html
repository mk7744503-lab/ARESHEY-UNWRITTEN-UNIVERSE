Some feelings are too big for ordinary words. So I made them a universe of their own.

TAKE YOUR TIME

01 · A little reminder

Before you see
anything else...

I hope you never forget how special you are.

Not because somebody says it. Not because somebody chooses you. But because your worth was never something you needed another person to give you.

02 · The things that matter

There is beauty
in being yourself.

Your little expressions.

Your unique way of speaking.

Your laughter, your voice, your little world.

Things that may feel ordinary to you can be unforgettable to someone else.

Never change your light just because somebody cannot see it.

03 · What I wish for you

A world you
truly deserve.

I hope you get happiness that stays.

People who respect your heart.

Dreams that become real.

Love that never makes you question your worth.

And a life where you never have to become less of yourself to be accepted.

04 · Something from my heart

Things I never
knew how to say.

I don't know how to explain every feeling. But I know that you became someone special in my world. I wanted to make something that could tell you what ordinary conversations never quite could. I don't expect anything from you. I just wanted to open my heart, honestly and without hiding. Your happiness matters. Your dreams matter. And you should never think of yourself as less than you are.

05 · Your story is yours

I won't write
your story for you.

Maybe our paths will be different. Maybe life has something else written for you.

Whatever you choose, your choice deserves respect.

I only wanted you to know what you have meant in my story.

One last little thing
ARESHEY

This little universe
was always yours.

Not because the universe needed a name.

But because this one needed a reason.

Never forget: you are special. Always.
